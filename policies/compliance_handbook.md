# Global Wealth & Banking Policy Handbook

## Section 1: Temporary Credit Limit Increases
- Premier Wealth clients are eligible for temporary credit limit increases for travel purposes up to RM 50,000.
- Standard Retail clients are capped at RM 10,000 for temporary limit modifications.
- Any temporary credit limit modification must strictly specify a 30-day expiry date from the activation date.

## Section 2: Foreign Transaction (FX) Fee Waivers
- Premier Wealth clients qualify for global foreign transaction (FX) transaction fee waivers on selected travel destinations.
- When granting any FX fee waivers, regulatory compliance mandates that the Relationship Manager (RM) must communicate the mandatory "FX Conversion Risk Disclosure Statement" directly to the client.
- Standard Retail clients are not eligible for travel-related fee waivers.
